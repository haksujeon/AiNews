## Who we are

AI NEWS is a Chinese AI news briefing service run by aibaseclub.com. Chinese media publish a flood of AI technology, product and business stories every day; we organize them so readers in Korean, English and Chinese can scan them quickly.

## Outlets we cover

We currently collect publicly available articles from these six Chinese outlets:

- 量子位 (QbitAI)
- 36氪 (36Kr)
- IT之家 (ITHome)
- 虎嗅 (Huxiu)
- 机器之心 (Synced)
- 新智元 (XinZhiYuan)

When we add or remove an outlet, we update this page.

## What we do

- Provide each article's title, a short summary, key terms and an AI-written insight in each language.
- Show only a 3–5 sentence gist of the article body, always with a link to the original.
- Publish a daily briefing and a weekly report on the day's main stories. A briefing is published only after it passes two separate AI reviews (weekly and monthly reports covering days with incomplete collection are published after an operator checks them).
- Record every withdrawn or replaced briefing in the [corrections log](/corrections).

## What we don't do

- We do not republish full articles. Please read the full text at the original outlet.
- We do not vouch for facts on behalf of the original outlets. Summaries and insights are written by AI from the original and can be wrong.
- We do not give investment advice or promote companies. Promotional items (event and recruiting notices, etc.) are ranked lower.

## AI disclosure

Translations, summaries, insights and briefing text on this site are produced by generative AI. Each page marks AI-written content, and the full process is described in our [methodology](/methodology).
