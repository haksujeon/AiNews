AI NEWS (the "Service") processes personal data as described below, in compliance with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws. You do not need an account or any personal data to read articles or briefings. This policy mainly applies to the email newsletter and to inquiries.

## 1. Data we process

- **Newsletter subscription (required):** company, name, email address, preferred language
- **Newsletter subscription (optional):** job title, topics of interest
- **Signup source:** which QR code or page the signup came from
- **Consent records:** time of consent, policy version consented to, IP address at the time of consent
- **Delivery records:** send time and result (delivered, bounced, unsubscribed)
- **Inquiries:** your email address and the content of your message

## 2. Purposes

- Sending the newsletter and managing subscriptions (confirmation, language and topic settings, unsubscribes)
- Proving consent and handling disputes
- Answering inquiries and handling error and correction requests
- Maintaining delivery quality, such as handling bounces and spam complaints

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or sale to other services.

## 3. Retention and deletion

- Subscription data is kept while you are subscribed and **deleted 30 days after you unsubscribe**.
- Consent records (time, version, IP) are **kept for 3 years** as evidence and then deleted.
- Inquiries are deleted within one year after they are resolved.
- Electronic files are erased irrecoverably, and backups are purged on the same schedule.

## 4. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide your personal data to third parties, except where lawfully required, for example by investigative authorities.

## 5. Processors

We entrust newsletter delivery to the following processor:

- **Processor:** Google LLC (Gmail) — we will update this if the provider changes
- **Tasks:** sending emails and processing delivery and bounce results
- **Transfers abroad:** the provider's servers may be located outside Korea. Once the provider is chosen, we will list the destination country, the timing and method of transfer, and the retention period here.

## 6. Your rights

You can at any time request access to, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal data, and withdraw your consent.

- Every newsletter includes an **unsubscribe** link at the bottom.
- For other requests, write to [{{contactEmail}}](mailto:{{contactEmail}}); we will act without delay.
- We do not accept subscriptions from children under 14.

## 7. Cookies and browser storage

- We do not use advertising or tracking cookies for readers.
- A session cookie is used only for administrator login.
- Your theme choice (dark/light) is stored only in your browser and is not sent to our servers.

## 8. Security measures

- Access to personal data is limited to the operators.
- All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
- Database access rights are separated and access is logged.

When we change this policy, we announce it on this page 7 days before it takes effect and bump the version string. Newsletter subscribers are also notified by email.
